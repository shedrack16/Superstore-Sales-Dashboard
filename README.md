# Superstore Sales Data Analysis (Interactive Dashboard creation using Power BI)

## Project Objective
Superstore wants to create a sales performance report covering 2015-2018. So that, the business can understand its customers, sales trends, and top-performing regions/products to grow more sales going forward.

## Dataset used
- [Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/divaadelia/superstore-sales-data?resource=download)

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the total sales, average order value, and total number of orders?
- What is the year-over-year (YoY) sales growth?
- How have sales trended over time (2015-2018)?
- How do sales compare across customer segments - Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office?
- Who are the top customers by total sales?
- Which product category and sub-category generate the most sales?
- Which states and regions contribute the most to sales?

## Process
- Verified data for any missing values and anomalies, and sorted out the same.
- Made sure data was consistent and clean with respect to data type, data format, and values used.
- Built KPI cards, trend charts, and comparison visuals according to the questions asked.
- Merged all visuals into one dashboard and applied slicers (City, Region) to make it dynamic.

## Project Insight
- Total sales reached $1.11bn from 4,922 orders, with an average order value of $226.5K.
- Sales grew 15% year-over-year.
- Sales trended steadily upward from 2015 through 2018.
- Consumer segment drives the majority of sales (52%), followed by Corporate (30%) and Home Office (18%).
- William Brown is the top customer by sales ($3.94M), followed by Yana Sorensen and Zuschuss Carroll.
- Furniture is the top-selling category, followed by Technology and Office Supplies.
- Bookcases and Chairs are the leading sub-categories.
- California and Texas are the top-selling states, with the Central and East regions contributing the most.

## Final Conclusion:
To improve Superstore's sales, a strategic focus on the Consumer segment in California and Texas should be implemented, since these represent the largest revenue drivers. The approach should include prioritizing Furniture and Technology inventory, replicating the success of top-performing customer relationships, and expanding marketing efforts in the Central and East regions where growth potential is highest.
